Contribution guidelines

  • Share first-hand experiences whenever possible.
  • Include the approximate hiring year or batch.
  • Separate confirmed facts from personal opinions.
  • Never publish confidential or personally identifying information.
  • Be respectful and constructive.
Trust grows when information is transparent about its source and date.
